Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This is a starting template, not legal advice — have it reviewed by a lawyer familiar with the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) before publishing.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, phone number, and email address. Riders additionally provide a profile photo, driver's license (front and back), and motorbike plate number and color for verification. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and never stored by us in plain text.

2. Location Data

While an order is active, we collect live GPS location from the assigned rider (and, separately, from the customer) to power real-time tracking on both sides and to estimate arrival times. Riders' live location is also used to match nearby pending orders. Location sharing stops once a delivery is completed or cancelled.

3. Order, Chat & Payment Details

We store pickup/drop-off addresses, delivery notes, fares, and in-app chat messages between customers and riders, so both sides have a record of the delivery. For wallet refills, we collect the GCash reference number and sender name you provide to help an administrator manually verify the transfer — we do not have access to your GCash account itself.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to operate the App: matching customers with riders, verifying rider applications, calculating fares, processing wallet refills, providing customer support, and keeping the platform safe (e.g. investigating complaints or suspicious activity).

5. Who We Share It With

A customer can see their assigned rider's name, vehicle details, and live location during a delivery; a rider can see the customer's pickup/drop-off location and name. We don't sell your personal information. We use third-party services to run the App, including Firebase/Google Cloud (hosting, authentication, database), Cloudinary (photo storage for rider documents), and OpenStreetMap/Nominatim (address search and map display) — each processes data only as needed to provide their part of the service.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures (including Firebase's security rules and authentication) to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct inaccuracies, to object to certain processing, and to request deletion, subject to legal exceptions. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise these rights, contact us through the Support tab or at [add contact email].

9. Children's Privacy

Padala Go is not intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Reach us through the Support tab in the App, or at [add contact email].
